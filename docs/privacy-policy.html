Namma Meter — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Namma Meter ("the app") is an auto-rickshaw fare meter and fare estimator. It is designed to work without any server. This policy explains what the app does with your data.

Summary

  • Your location is used only on your phone, only while the meter is running or when you ask for your current position.
  • The app has no account, no login, no ads, no analytics and no third-party trackers.
  • We do not collect, sell or share any personal data. The developer never receives your data.

Location

When you press START, the app reads your precise GPS location (about once per second) to measure trip distance and waiting time. This continues while the screen is off or another app is open, and a notification is shown for the whole trip. Tracking stops when you press STOP.

The app does not request "background location" permission. It does not track you when the meter is not running.

When you tap "Show my location" or pick a destination on the map, the app reads your current location once.

Data stored on your phone

  • Trip history: start/end time, distance, waiting time and fare breakdown. The route itself is not saved after the trip ends.
  • Settings: fare tariff, language, meter settings, and — if you use driver mode — your UPI ID, payee name and a salted hash of your PIN (the PIN itself is not stored).
  • The in-progress trip, so it can be resumed if the app is closed unexpectedly.

This data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer. Uninstalling the app deletes it. You can delete individual trips in the app.

Data that leaves your phone (only if you use these optional features)

  • OpenRouteService — only if you enter your own API key and ask for a road-distance estimate. The start and destination coordinates are sent to openrouteservice.org (HeiGIT gGmbH) under their privacy policy. Nothing else is sent.
  • Map tiles — only if you open the in-app map. Your phone downloads map images from the tile server set in Settings (by default OpenStreetMap). As with any web request, that server sees your IP address and the map area requested.
  • Google Maps / browser — "Show my location" and "Plan a trip" open Google Maps (or your browser) with the location or destination you chose. What happens there is governed by Google's privacy policy.
  • Sharing — when you tap Share, the fare text goes to the app you choose.
  • UPI QR — the QR code is generated on the phone. The passenger's UPI app reads it; Namma Meter takes no part in the payment.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data about anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address and in the app's About screen.
